Choose an editable brief saved from this Workbench. Its references are checked before anything in this form is replaced.

1. Define the decision

State a claim that an observation could support or challenge. For example: “Every Safari checkout fails.”

2. Link your observations

Add the references you plan to cite, such as “Public report A, page 2”. For each observation, choose its source and decide whether it supports the hypothesis, challenges it, or just adds context.

Import observations from CSV

Use a UTF-8 CSV with observation, source_reference, source_type, and role columns. Use fictional or public for source_type, and supports, challenges, or context for role. Add new rows to keep your work, or replace all source and observation rows. Both options keep your decision text.
